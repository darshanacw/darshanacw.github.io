Classification method
Each country is classified using the 10-year mean of annual P, PET and AET for the selected range.

P = Precipitation (mm/year)
PET = Potential Evapotranspiration (mm/year)
AET = Actual Evapotranspiration (mm/year)

The map contains six classes representing the six possible rank orders of P, PET and AET.
