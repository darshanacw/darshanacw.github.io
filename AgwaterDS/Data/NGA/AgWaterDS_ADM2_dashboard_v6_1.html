Crop Water Requirement (CWR)
Water the crop uses to grow well, ETo × Kc. It stays above 0 while the crop is growing.
Effective rainfall
Share of rainfall the crop can use (80% of rain, never more than the CWR).
Irrigation need
(CWR − effective rainfall) × efficiency factor. It is 0 in months when rain already meets the CWR. It is the gross amount that irrigation must deliver.
Irrigation supply (total supply)
Usable surface water first, then groundwater, never more than the irrigation need.
Gap-Demand
Irrigation need − irrigation supply. Water that is needed but not available.
GM (Growing Month)
GM1 starts in the most common season-start month of the ADM2 (shown as a calendar month). Each GM is a successive 30-day crop period; the last GM can be shorter.
Chart lines
Core lines: irrigation need, irrigation supply, gap. Optional lines: CWR, effective rainfall, surface water, groundwater, for investigating why the signal rises or falls.
mm and MCM
mm is the average depth over the crop area of the ADM2. MCM is the total volume in million m³.
