Crop Water Requirement (CWR)
Water the crop uses to grow well, ETo × Kc. It stays above 0 while the crop is growing.
Effective rainfall
Share of rainfall the crop can use (80% of rain, never more than the CWR).
Irrigation need
(CWR − effective rainfall) × efficiency factor. It is 0 in months when rain already meets the CWR. It is the gross amount that irrigation must deliver.
Irrigation supply (total supply)
Each growing month: surface water first, then groundwater from the storage that is left. Never more than the irrigation need.
Groundwater storage
One fixed annual stock per cell: the 12 monthly groundwater values added up, times the usable share. GM1 uses what surface water cannot meet from this stock; the storage is reduced by that use; GM2 compares its need with what is left, and so on. A month has no gap while the storage covers it. Each crop-season is an independent planning scenario that starts with the full annual stock; other crops, the other season and other users are not accounted for.
Gap-Demand
Irrigation need − irrigation supply. Water that is needed but not available.
GM (Growing Month)
GM1 starts in the most common season-start month of the ADM2 (shown as a calendar month). Each GM is a successive 30-day crop period; the last GM can be shorter.
Chart lines
Core lines: irrigation need, irrigation supply, gap. Optional lines: CWR, effective rainfall, surface water, groundwater, groundwater storage left, for investigating why the signal rises or falls.
mm and MCM
mm is the average depth over the crop area of the ADM2. MCM is the total volume in million m³.
